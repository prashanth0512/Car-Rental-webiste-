PLAN INQUIRIES

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before choosing your rental plan.

Our Daily Drive plan includes 24 hours of self-drive freedom, standard comprehensive insurance, 24/7 live roadside assistance, sanitization, and flexible vehicle category selection. Standard pickup is available from our atelier or airport hub.
The Weekly Explorer package provides approximately 22% savings compared to standard daily rates, complimentary interstate FasTag toll support, doorstep handover at your residence or hotel, and one free reservation reschedule without penalty.
Yes. Our Monthly Freedom plan delivers our highest per-day value with substantial discounts over short-term rentals. It includes monthly vehicle swap privileges, complimentary scheduled sanitization maintenance, and dedicated account concierge.
Extending your reservation is effortless. Simply request an extension through your online customer dashboard or call our 24/7 concierge at least 4 hours before your scheduled return time. Extensions are confirmed based on fleet availability.
Yes. You may modify your vehicle selection up to 24 hours before your scheduled pickup without modification fees. Upgrading to a higher-tier vehicle (such as a Luxury SUV or Executive Sedan) will simply adjust the rate difference.
We operate on a transparent "Same-to-Same" fuel policy. Your vehicle is delivered with a full tank of fuel and should be returned with a matching fuel level. If returned with less fuel, local market fuel charges apply without penal markups.
We charge zero hidden administration fees. A refundable security deposit authorization is placed on your credit/debit card upon vehicle collection (ranging from ₹5,000 for Economy up to ₹15,000 for Luxury) and released within 48 hours after vehicle return.
Enjoy peace of mind with our flexible cancellation policy. Cancellations submitted 24 hours or more before pickup receive a 100% full refund with zero cancellation charges. Cancellations within 24 hours may incur a nominal one-day reservation holding fee.
